COSTA SOL

A sunny city. A heavy debt. One getaway driver.

Lena Cruz drives for a living — and owes Marco Venn $2,000 for the last job that went wrong. Nine jobs stand between her and a clean slate: fares, a borrowed coupe, a run from the law, a hot package, a quick score, one last getaway — and then the reckoning: the van, the evidence, and Marco himself. The whole city is open: walk it, drive it, steal it. Day turns to night, districts fall under her control, and the streets offer races, vigilante chases and paramedic runs between jobs.
